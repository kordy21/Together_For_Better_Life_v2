Implementation of the Bab Rizk Karim Program Project

22 october

Cases in need of small projects (sewing machines) were identified among poor families and female-headed households (divorced, widowed, and abandoned women) in the villages of Al-Wasta Center, Beni Suef Governorate. Subsequently, 7 projects were delivered to 7 beneficiaries, each consisting of: a semi-computerized sewing machine, "Sancir" model, and a serger machine, "Jo Co" model, which are among the best types available in the Egyptian market according to the beneficiaries’ preferences, with 2 machines provided per beneficiary.
