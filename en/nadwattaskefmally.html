Financial Literacy Seminar

26 October

In line with the financial inclusion and savings initiatives and under the auspices of the Central Bank of Egypt, Attijariwafa Bank Egypt organized a financial literacy seminar in cooperation with the "Together for a Better Life" Foundation for a group of women at the Women’s Club in Bebshtil, Oseem district, Giza governorate. Another seminar was held in collaboration with Nasser Youth Center in Zagazig, Sharqia governorate.
